# Beyond the File author notes

**Audience:** campaign authors and evaluators. [Starting lives and actor text](README.md)

A regional sandbox runs on persistent people, places and commitments. Each start gives the same region a different point of entry. The player may take work, keep contact with others, look for shelter, ask for care, investigate a report, or negotiate limits over time. An institution may answer, refuse, defer or offer conditions. Its response must follow its own information, authority and resources.

## Region and starting scope

The **Old Ferry Stop** and **public depot** open to the public walk. The walk connects to the **Quay Clinic** and the outside of the **Quay Annex**. The Annex is a staffed Foundation outpost. Its controlled rooms and service passage toward **Site Kestrel** need separate access. This is authored adjacency for future mapping. Route availability and travel time require a compatible profile. A distant view conveys what is observable from there, including the fact that someone is not visible rather than an invented absence.

The field agent has a limited assignment and contact, not a universal entry credential. The field scientist can ask to view, copy or borrow a record. Permission for one of those actions is not permission for the others. Tovan starts in custody on a defined supervised visit. They can ask for care, privacy, a different approved destination or review of a restriction. Treat the Foundation's designation as a record with scope. It is not the actor's identity and does not remove their choices. Requests can meet real refusals without silently changing custody or access.

The openings use names and locations already known to their selected actor. An external observer sees what they can perceive or what someone actually shares. New consent, permission, recognition and custody are durable records only when the appropriate authority commits them.

## Anomaly starts

| Sable, independent | Produce a short audible message on the opposite side of the same closed door while in contact with that frame. The effect requires a valid local doorway and an admissible action. | The Bell, held at the Quay Annex | Sense direct contact, near sound and movement of the body. Sound a note when the body can move as required. An enclosure may damp or block that sound. The actor cannot read an exterior label or walk out of the case. | Use agreed signals, establish new communication with a willing keeper, and request a change of place or terms. The Bell's opening agreement gives two short notes, chosen as a single signal, the limited meaning “more time.” The single attention note and silence carry no approval. A transfer proposal can be declined or held even if a keeper could physically carry the case. The two anomaly abilities require selected Engine capabilities. Until qualification, the examples are proposed effects rather than outcomes the Catalog can claim.

## Play across sessions

Start from one default introduction. Continue from actual state rather than requiring a secret encounter to begin play. A refused request can lead to a later appointment, a different place or an independent routine. If the player accepts an appointment, another character may honor or change it. If a transfer, shift or rest occurs, show its actual time, costs and continuing conditions. The environment and nonplayer characters move forward only through real Core operations.

After a committed choice, the next exchange can stay small. If the keeper receives the pause signal and decides to honor it, their line is “I can leave the case open a little longer.” If the caretaker receives Sable’s greeting and elects to ask the speaker to wait, they might say “Stay where I can see you. I’m finding the key.” Neither line commits a move or an agreement to enter.

The first human and anomaly playtests should prove different lived constraints. For the supervised start, demonstrate an approved visit or refused route, an authorized alternative, and the saved custody record. For Sable, demonstrate a valid local sound and a refused remote attempt with no extra sound. For the Bell, demonstrate a valid signal, a real response or explicit absence of one, and a resumed location after a permitted or refused move. Retrying after interruption must preserve the previous result and avoid repeating sound or travel.

The [character creation draft](../character-creation.md) defines the proposed menu paths and custom anomaly review. The first custom start needs a validated body, senses, supported abilities and an eligible starting situation. A name or class assignment alone cannot fill any of those fields.

Engine owns travel, needs, access and custody scopes, permissions, character decisions, and the proposed embodiment, perception and signal rules. App owns selection, capabilities in the reviewed action flow, role-appropriate information and recovery. Catalog owns the authored choices and independent expected consequences. Evaluate the human and anomaly starts against exact selected sources before offering a launch.
