# Create a character

Choose a campaign, then choose whose life you want to play. Each menu shows the choices available for the selections above it. The examples on this page are a proposed WorldWard creation flow for the Catalog campaign drafts.

## Play as an anomaly

**Campaign → Play as → Category → Containment class → Anomaly → Review character**

**Category** describes the kind of being or object you want to play, such as an anomalous person or a sentient object. **Containment class** filters the roster by the Foundation's classification where the campaign permits it to be shown. Safe, Euclid and Keter are examples of classifications about containment. They do not set a power level or measure how dangerous the character is. **Unclassified** is available where a start has no disclosed classification. [SCP Wiki Object Classes](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/object-classes)

| Campaign | Category | Visible class | Anomaly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beyond the File | Anomalous person | Unclassified | [Sable](beyond-the-file/README.md#independent-anomaly), who can send words through a closed door |
| Beyond the File | Sentient object | Euclid, proposed for this draft | [The Bell](beyond-the-file/README.md#anomaly-in-foundation-custody), a brass bell that communicates in notes |

Selecting an anomaly leads to its reviewed start, including its senses, abilities and situation. Containment class and custody are separate. For example, Sable lives independently and the Bell begins in Foundation custody. Beyond the File is the first candidate for anomaly selection and custom creation. Last Confirmed and The Kestrel Watch have personnel openings in this draft.

## Form your Anomaly

Choose **Form your Anomaly** as an alternative to selecting a preset. It opens its own page directly from **Play as an anomaly**, so you can start writing without first choosing a class. Two multiline text boxes invite the player to describe the character.

| Field | Prompt shown to the player |
| --- | --- |
| **What does your anomaly look like?** | Describe its body, form, or how someone nearby would notice it. |
| **What makes it anomalous?** | Describe what it can do, when it happens, and what might stop it. |

Choose **Interpret abilities** to preview a draft. The appearance stays as written and may suggest a category. Show each proposed ability with its trigger, target or range, limits, and what happens when a requirement is missing. A proposal should distinguish abilities the selected campaign can support from details that need editing or can be kept as description. The player can edit the original text, adjust or remove an ability, and review the result before choosing an available starting situation and confirming creation.

For example, a player describes a palm-sized glazed figurine and writes, “I can sound a short note from inside my body, and I hear every conversation in town.” A **Local chime** proposal produces one sound at the figurine's actual location when its body can sound. Someone hears it only if they are in reach of the sound. Sound may be muffled or blocked by an enclosing case. **Hear every conversation in town** stays unsupported in the proposed profile. The player can describe it as a rumor without gaining information, remove it, or revise the idea to hearing nearby speech through enabled senses. The chime can be offered as a playable ability once the selected Core profile supports it.

## Review and creation

Changing a campaign clears incompatible branches, roles or presets. Changing a personnel type clears the old role. Changing an anomaly category or class clears any incompatible preset. Keep typed anomaly prose during navigation, but require a fresh ability interpretation when the text, campaign or capability selection changes. Keep downstream menus inactive until their prerequisite is chosen. Give each menu a visible label and explain when a campaign has no eligible choices.

The creation preview contains proposed selections and effects only. The App obtains the eligible starts and capabilities from the selected Engine sources and offers a clear confirmation step. The Engine validates each ability and starting entitlement, keeps unavailable effects out of the character, and commits the confirmed creation through the existing Core. Prose and any interpretation output are proposals. They grant neither permissions nor powers. If an interpretation service is unavailable, keep the text and let the player select and edit supported abilities manually. If a start or preview becomes stale, keep the description and ask the player to review the refreshed choices before committing again. A retry after a successful commit recovers the original character.

The Catalog supplies the role paths, roster copy, source attribution and these examples. The App owns the actual creation flow. The Engine owns compatible starting situations, ability validation and effects. Before enabling a start, verify that campaign and type changes clear stale choices, unsupported prose cannot create mechanics, and a confirmed creation restores the chosen role and approved abilities after reload. Verify one actual ability effect and one blocked attempt in a synthetic playtest.
