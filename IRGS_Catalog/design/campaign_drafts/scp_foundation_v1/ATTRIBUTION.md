# SCP Foundation campaign credits

This collection adapts the general **SCP Foundation** setting and institutional terminology by the SCP Wiki community. The collection adds original scenarios, locations, characters, anomalies and dialogue for the WorldWard Catalog. The draft draws on common Foundation concepts. It incorporates no individual SCP entry, named canon character, existing game asset or SCP logo.

Credit the SCP Wiki community and the WorldWard Catalog contributors, link the applicable sources and license, and indicate changes when reusing or adapting the material.
