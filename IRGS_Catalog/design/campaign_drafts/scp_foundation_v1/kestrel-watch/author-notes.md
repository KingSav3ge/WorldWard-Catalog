# The Kestrel Watch author notes

**Audience:** campaign authors and evaluators. [Role selection and openings](README.md)

This is a persistent site campaign. The same controlled person can finish a watch, save, and return to work with prior choices in force. Balance ordinary duties, care, rest and relationships as well as exceptional events. The fixed site concentrates resources and people. Its institutions can decline, reschedule and change priorities through their own authorized decisions.

## Starting situation

The controlled roles begin within Site Kestrel in the research bench room, common room, duty office, crossing post, maintenance desk and clinic. Room Two is a separate interview room with a reported call signal fault. The open clinic room is different. The supplied work ticket and conflicting chime reports document a complaint and prior testimony. A reader should not infer a failed part. Rina holds one sealed spare. The first watch has one escorted crossing available. One clinic room and one crossing are two different capacities. Lio has requested a private conversation. Escort, assessment, treatment and any research participation each require their own grounds.

An occupied corridor requires a valid escort assignment in this authored start. The clinic review and equipment inspection compete for the one available slot. Contact with the clinic from the current wing and work at the bench are separate options. Any reserve, release and actual crossing must be recorded through existing owners. The work ticket permits review of the repair request. It is not an allocation, proof of a diagnosis or authorization to spend the spare.

Select the opening for the chosen person only. The overview and other opening perspectives are reading previews. They are not starting knowledge. Other people remain able to make decisions according to the information they actually possess.

## The next watch

Use a handoff to carry forward unfinished work, rather than a reset to the opening. The next watch can receive items with sources and follow up. Continuation examples are eligible when those records exist.

- Lio spoke by the line and declined to travel. The next medic can offer another time if authorized. Lio stays in the recorded location and nobody consumes the unused crossing.
- Rina carried out an authorized inspection and the result did not identify a failed module. The original work ticket stays open or receives the recorded disposition. The spare is still in its actual custody. The next technician can see what was tested.
- The duty desk deferred both last-place requests. The next watch gets unresolved decisions and their reasons. Staff later set fresh capacity through the actual roster. Passing a boundary does not create an extra crossing in the earlier watch.
- A repair was completed with an actual material issue and work result. The next watch sees the changed room status and inventory. Interruption, restart or retry preserves the original result and does not issue another spare.

When a verified inspection ends without a diagnosis, Rina can leave a handoff in their own words: “I heard the call from the room. I couldn’t confirm it at the desk. The spare is still sealed.” Use that wording only when those are the committed findings and disposition.

Typical later opportunities include relief coverage, confidential space, interrupted work and staff trying to rearrange a schedule. Staff can protect a commitment, decline extra work, agree to relief, or suggest another time. Present fatigue, rest or recovery only from actual role needs and resource state. Avoid setting a universal watch length or a civil time from the handoff label.

Engine must support work prerequisites, time precision, needs and recovery, finite capacity, material use, ordinary refusals and durable handoff records. App must restore the same actor and scope, retain the player's unfinished intention, and show the original result after retry. Catalog acceptance should run at least two watches from ordinary role selection, including one declined request, one actual state change, and an interrupted return. A planned watch is not evidence that these features ran.
