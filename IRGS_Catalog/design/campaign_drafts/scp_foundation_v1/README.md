# SCP Foundation campaigns

Three unofficial WorldWard campaign drafts set in the fictional Alder Quay district and its nearby Foundation facility, Site Kestrel. Choose a campaign, then choose whose life you enter.

| Campaign | What you play |
| --- | --- |
| [Last Confirmed](last-confirmed/README.md) | A bounded containment incident. A transfer form places someone in two destinations while they are still waiting to leave. Play through the disputed handoff from one of seven positions. |
| [The Kestrel Watch](kestrel-watch/README.md) | Ongoing life at a fixed Foundation site. Take a shift, deal with the work and people in reach, and return to see what your decisions left for the next watch. |
| [Beyond the File](beyond-the-file/README.md) | Open-world life across Alder Quay. Keep a Foundation assignment, live under Foundation custody, or play an anomaly with its own body, senses and choices. |

Each campaign starts a separate story. Site Kestrel can feature in several campaigns, with a fresh starting situation and its own later history in each. Choose one role for a playthrough. The other people keep their own responsibilities and choices.

The [character creation draft](character-creation.md) shows the proposed personnel and anomaly menus and the Form your Anomaly page.

The linked pages are written openings. Each lets you read from one perspective and pick or write an intention. They are a Catalog concept draft awaiting WorldWard playtest support.

The [SCP Wiki source guide](source-guide.md) brings together the four reference hubs and gives campaign authors draft examples.

The Foundation setting and role terminology come from the SCP Wiki. The district, site, people, anomalies and scenes were created for this collection. [Credits and license](ATTRIBUTION.md)
