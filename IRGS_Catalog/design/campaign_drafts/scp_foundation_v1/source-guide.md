# SCP Wiki source guide and writing samples

These four hubs serve different parts of the WorldWard SCP collection. The writing below is original to Site Kestrel and Alder Quay. It gives authors examples to review alongside the existing campaign openings.

| Wiki reference | What to take from it |
| --- | --- |
| [SCP Article Hub](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-hub) | Browse by containment class and attribute. For a preset candidate, read its own article and record what the anomaly does, what triggers it, its limits, and what a particular character can know about it. |
| [Foundation Tales](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/foundation-tales) | Browse genres and series for narrative approaches. Use this lane to develop character voices, ordinary scenes, and consequences that unfold after the incident. |
| [Artwork Hub](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/artwork-hub) | Browse artists, art pages, comics and styles when developing visual direction. Record the original asset page, artist and license if proposing a particular image for use. |
| [Groups of Interest Formats](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/goi-formats) | Read documents written from other organizations’ points of view. An outside group can have its own interests, limits and terms, and can disagree with a Foundation account of the same event. |

## Anomaly records for Last Confirmed

A player can discover one fact at a time. The proposed file uses the same organization as the [incident draft](last-confirmed/author-notes.md).

| Record | Original draft copy | When to show it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observation entry | “K-041 is visible on a form behind the receiving tray glass. Destination on the form reads Transit Hall.” | The chosen character can see and read that form or receives a clearly attributed account of it. |
| Research note | “Both sheets carry K-041. The destinations differ. Keep both until the source of each can be established.” | The researcher actually reviews both records and commits a note, or the actor receives those recorded words. |
| Author explanation | The Second Copy can create a physical document with a conflicting destination in the sealed tray. Its effect creates a record that can be observed. The person's location follows the actual movement state. | Keep with the authored start and evaluation material. A player learns only the evidence their character receives. |

If a Wiki anomaly is later considered for the [preset roster](character-creation.md#play-as-an-anomaly), the Catalog entry needs its exact source and credited author, a campaign-specific version and limits, and a reviewed starting body or form. The Engine determines whether its abilities and consequences are supported. The Wiki tag is research input for the category and containment class menus.

## A scene for The Kestrel Watch

After a clinic call, a worker setting out cups finds yesterday’s duty sheet still clipped to the board. The space beside Lio’s appointment is wide enough for one word. The new medic sets the sheet on the table and turns it so Lio can read it.

“I can give you another line if you need one.”

Lio picks up the pencil. “I wanted to talk. I wanted to stay here.”

Use this continuation when the relevant people are present, Lio actually accepted the private conversation and declined travel, and those words are authorized for the exchange. If the player controls Lio, leave the line open for what the player chooses to say. The next shift gets only the handoff that was actually recorded, as in the [site campaign draft](kestrel-watch/author-notes.md#the-next-watch).

## Visual direction for the collection

- **Last Confirmed.** Show the narrow space between two local documents bearing the same reference. Give each destination its own legible line. Keep the image close to the character’s vantage point so it does not reveal where the missing person really is.
- **The Kestrel Watch.** Use ordinary care and work details. A taped repair tag, a stack of rinsed cups, and a pencil shortened between shifts put people in the facility.
- **Beyond the File.** Use a damp bus shelter and the ferry office after closing. Keep the public walk, clinic and Annex visually distinct. A sign or window can show where a character could ask about entry without implying they have access.

These briefs describe original compositions. The Artwork Hub references are research links. Any proposed asset needs its own provenance and rights record before it appears in a campaign or in character creation.

## An outside voice for Beyond the File

**Alder Quay Neighbors’ Desk, draft public notice**

> If you need somewhere dry while the depot is open, ask at our table. Tell us what you need a room for and how long you expect to stay. If we can’t offer a place, we’ll tell you who else is on duty. You can give us the name you use here.

**Foundation field note, separate draft**

> Confirmed a community contact at the depot. The contact offered to discuss short stays. Ask who manages the room and what terms apply before recording a placement.

The Neighbors’ Desk is an original community group for this draft. The notice may be read at the depot if it is present and visible in the selected start. The field note needs an actual contact and recorded exchange. A room offer in play needs a decision and available capacity. Either account can give the actor a reason to ask next, while the actual stays, obligations and refusals follow the [sandbox’s continuing play](beyond-the-file/author-notes.md#play-across-sessions).
